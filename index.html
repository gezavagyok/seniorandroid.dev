Works fine today. But it pins the coroutine to Main, which means this can't be unit tested without a dispatcher rule, and it'll silently break the moment repo.fetch needs a background thread. Let the scope's default dispatcher do its job.

The actual gap

Three things nobody puts in a job description

Ownership

Outcomes, not tickets

A mid-level dev closes the ticket. A senior dev asks whether the ticket was the right one, and owns what happens after it ships.

Trade-offs

Principles, not rules

SOLID, clean architecture, DI — they're tools with costs, not commandments. Seniors know which ones to break, and can defend why.

Judgment

Comfort with ambiguity

The spec is incomplete on purpose. Senior engineers fill the gaps with reasoning instead of waiting for someone to hand them certainty.
